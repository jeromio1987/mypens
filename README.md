# MY PENS

> The trusted interpretation layer between raw trackers and everyday users.

---

## What is this?

MY PENS is a weight and health tracking app built around one idea: **raw data is noisy — people deserve signal, not jargon.**

Most trackers show you a number. MY PENS explains what it means.

A weekend trip, a salty meal, a hard workout — your scale doesn't know any of that. MY PENS does.

---

## Who it's for

- Ordinary people who like tracking but get confused by daily weight swings
- Slightly sportive users who want meaningful signal without becoming biohackers
- People who want to understand their body, not manage a spreadsheet

**Not for:** hardcore biohackers, broad wellness dashboards, or people who want to sync everything.

---

## Core idea

**One product. Two depths.**

- Default mode: easy tracking, clear explanations, minimal setup
- Advanced mode: local-first controls, deeper source management, richer interpretation

No split apps. One data model. Trust before convenience. Convenience before scale.
